Key analytical finding from the table

Serbia's MAJRES scores across four episodes are 3 → 7 → 3 → 7. The pattern is reactive, not aggressive: Serbia alternates between political capitulation and combined diplomatic-military response depending on whether it faces a Great Power or a weaker adversary. It never scores 8 (pure violent military initiation) in any episode where the adversary is a Great Power. This is your quantitative weak-state argument.
